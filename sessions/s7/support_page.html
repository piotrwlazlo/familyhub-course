If you’re struggling, you’re not alone. Don’t suffer in silence. Our family hubs are here to support you.

There’s also a range of services across Blackpool to suit your needs including out of hours hotlines and more.

Help with yours or your childs mental health

If you’re struggling with your mental health, Blackpool family hubs and our partners are here to support you. Here are a range of services below that you can access.

Advice and helplines

  • Mind – a national charity that gives advice on mental health. Phone: 0300 123 3393. Email: info@mind.org.uk Mind website
  • Childline – if you’re under 19, you can call the Childline mental health support line. The number won’t appear on your phone bill. Phone: 0800 1111
  • Every Mind Matters – a free mental health quiz that creates a personalised plan, with tips and advice to help you look after your mental health and wellbeing. Take the Every Mind Matters quiz
  • Samaritans – The Samaritans is a charity dedicated to preventing suicide. Whatever you're going through, a Samaritan will face it with you. They're available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Just call 116 123.

Support for young people's mental health

  • The Mix - We connect young people to experts and their peers to talk about everything from money to mental health, homelessness to jobs, break-ups to drugs and more. No topic is out of bounds, and we are completely non-judgemental. www.themix.org.uk
  • NSPCC – the NSPCC has a hub of resources for parents to balance their mental health, dealing with toddler tantrums and recognising signs of self-harm in young people. www.nspcc.org.uk/advice-for-families/
  • Young Minds - YoungMinds is a mental health charity for children, young people and their parents, making sure all young people can get the mental health support they deserve. www.youngminds.org.uk
  • Beat Eating Disorders - Eating disorders are serious mental illnesses, and if you think you may have one, you deserve help to get better. Learn more about how to ask for and what to expect from treatment, as well as how we can support you. www.beateatingdisorders.org.uk
  • Soluna (by Kooth) - Soluna by Kooth offers free, safe confidential mental health support for young people aged 10 to 25. Soluna is available as web-version and a mobile app (for over 13s), which can be accessed at: https://solunaapp.com/uk or via the App Store/Play Store.

Talking therapies and groups

  • Blackpool Talking Therapies – free psychological therapies for people aged 16 and over.
  • Lancashire Women – a local charity that runs talking therapy sessions for women in Blackpool. Wellbeing sessions are held in our hubs by referral only. Phone: 0300 330 1354 . Lancashire Women website: lancashirewomen.org
  • Elliot’s Place – a space for men, run by local men, to talk about mental health. It works to reduce the stigma that still surrounds mental health conversations, particularly in male-dominated environments, and encourages men to reach out and speak honestly about how life is for them.

Support after abuse or bereavement

  • Birchall Trust – counselling and support for survivors of rape, sexual abuse and sexualised violence, including adult counselling and specialist therapists for young people. Professionals can refer through the Birchall Trust website. Sessions can be held in our hubs. Email: enquiries@birchalltrust.org.uk. Birchall Trust website.
  • Solace – compassionate support for anyone affected by suicide or suspected suicide, with a varied weekly programme of support and activities. See the Solace weekly programme.
  • The Den – provides specialist, trauma-informed support to children and young people aged 3 years and above affected by domestic abuse. Contact them here.

Pregnancy and new parents

  • Tommy’s – a charity offering mental health support and advice for women during pregnancy. Phone: 0800 014 7800. Email: midwife@tommys.org. Tommy’s mental health and wellbeing
  • Mind Your Step walks – walks run by the hubs with a trained mental health professional. A relaxed place to talk with other new parents and share your experiences and feelings.

See when the next Mind Your Step walk is on by looking at our timetable.

  • Move and Meet walks – Active Blackpool is running a six-week walking course for pregnant women and new mums with children up to two years old. Each one-hour session includes a gentle, guided walk in the park led by a midwifery health trainer, friendly conversation, and advice on pregnancy, wellbeing, nutrition, exercise, routines and sleep. Join the sessions that suit you – you don’t need to come every week. Places are limited. To book, text MOVE followed by your name to 07467 444656

Help with money and debt

Managing a family budget can be hard. Don’t be afraid to ask us or our partners for help.

Advice and drop-ins at the hubs

  • Cost of living drop-ins – speak one to one with an expert member of staff about the difficulties you’re facing. They’ll advise you on solutions. See the timetable for the next session
  • Citizens Advice – regular drop-in sessions at our family hubs. See the timetable.
  • Green Doctor – advice to help you take control of your bills and save energy at home. This includes support if you worry about heating or eating, have a cold or damp home, or have breathing problems such as asthma or a persistent cough. Drop-ins are held in the hubs. See the timetable for the next drop-in.

Debt, savings and loans

  • National Debt Line – free advice about debt. Phone: 0800 808 4000. National Debt Line website.
  • CLEVR Money – a locally run, not-for-profit credit union that offers socially responsible savings and loans to families and individuals in our community. Look out for their next drop-in at our hubs. CLEVR Money website. Hub drop-in dates.

Food and financial help

  • Blackpool’s Big Food Truck – sign up as a member on the Blackpool Food Bank website, or text your name and postcode. The details you give won’t be shared with anyone outside the scheme. Text: 07860 027502. Blackpool Food Bank website.
  • Healthy Start card – you may be eligible if you’re more than 10 weeks pregnant or have a child under 4, and you receive certain benefits. The pre-paid card is topped up every four weeks so you can buy food and milk for children. Find out if you’re eligible
  • Crisis and resilience fund – Blackpool Council’s fund covers a range of situations for families. See the council’s crisis and resilience fund page, including how to apply for a crisis payment.

Help with drugs, alcohol and gambling

If you need help with a drug, alcohol or gambling problem, you’re entitled to help and support in the same way as anyone else with a health problem.

You can visit your GP or come into your local family hub. We can refer you to a range of free services in Blackpool. These are services we work with in Blackpool and beyond.

  • Horizon – funded by Blackpool Council to provide drug and alcohol support for all Blackpool residents. They offer information, health screenings, treatment (including opioid substitution therapy) and counselling. All confidential and free. Phone: 0800 6522206. Horizon drop-in dates and times on our timetable here.
  • Blackpool Recovery Hub – a relaxed, judgement-free space where everyone is welcome, whether you’re just beginning your journey or looking to maintain your wellbeing. Visit their website here to find out more.
  • Blackpool Adolescents: drug, alcohol and sexual health – if you’re aged 25 or under, or you’re a parent, carer, friend or professional, you can contact the service for help and advice. They can give advice over the phone or arrange one-to-one work. They host sessions in our Central Family Hub.
  • Project K – is a support group for people aged over 18 to access support with a Ketamine addiction. Facilitated by Horizon, you can find out more here.
  • Frank – everything you need to know about drugs, their effects and the law. Frank website.

Gambling support 

  • Empowerment – a local charity that runs support groups, including one for gambling harm. Whether you’re affected by your own gambling or someone else’s, they offer listening, guidance and practical support towards recovery. Gambling Harm - Empowerment Charity.
  • Lancashire Women - Whether gambling is affecting you directly or someone close to you, Lancashire Women offers free, confidential support for
    women across Lancashire. Visit their website here to find out more.

Help to stop smoking

Ready to quit smoking for good? There is free support and advice across Blackpool. Quit for you, quit for two!

  • If you’re pregnant – the Blackpool midwifery team holds regular appointments at your local family hub to support you. Book by email or phone. Phone: 01253 952226 . Email: bfwh.midwifery.healthtrainers@nhs.net. See the timetable for the next session near you.
  • Blackpool tobacco addiction service – drop-in sessions at all three Blackpool Family Hubs. See the timetable for dates and times.
  • Smokefree Blackpool Helpline – speak to an adviser for support and get free nicotine replacement therapy to help you quit. Phone: 0808 196 4324.

Housing help

If you’re struggling with housing issues, your local family hub can give you advice and point you to local services that may be able to help.

  • Renting in Blackpool – Blackpool Council has information for people who are renting or looking to rent. Problems with your landlord or rented property – support and advice is available. Click here to find out more.
  • Shelter – a national charity offering free support and advice on homelessness and getting back on your feet, including online resources, webchats and emergency helplines. Get help from Shelter.
  • Streetlife – local support for young people aged 16 to 25 in Blackpool, often those who are homeless. Support is offered from The Base, a day centre, and a night shelter that provides emergency accommodation for those who need it most. Workers are available for advice and support. Hot food, internet access, food parcels and more are available.

Drop-ins: Monday to Friday, 12:30pm to 3:30pm. The Base, 48 Buchanan Street, Blackpool, FY1 3LQ
