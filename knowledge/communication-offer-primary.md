# Communication offer - primary
URL: https://www.blackpool.gov.uk/Residents/Education-and-schools/Local-offer/Professionals/Communication-offer/Communication-offer-primary.aspx

## Universal

- Schools to secure continuing professional development
- [ICAN language development charts](http://www.speechandlanguage.org.uk)
- National curriculum guidance on speech and language
- A range of recorded training modules available for all staff covering all aspects of communication and interaction on Blackpool's Graduated Approach Toolkit
- [Access to The Communication Trust Self-Evaluation Framework for self-audit](http://www.speechandlanguage.org.uk)
- ELKLAN Let's Talk Level 1 training available from the Blackpool Council SEND support team
- ‘Introduction to speech, language and communication’ video for support staff from Blackpool's Graduated Approach toolkit
- Access to the communication leads in schools meetings, held termly
- Access to [Blackpool Classrooms that Communicate toolkit](https://sites.google.com/thespeechbubble.co.uk/bctc)
- Makaton training via NHS speech and language therapist payable by settings
- Basic BSL training, some charges may apply
- Autism Education Trust Making Sense of Autism for Schools

## Targeted

- Access to initiatives such as Talking Partners, Talk Boost, Time to Talk, Socially Speaking
- Elklan interventions and resources
- Access to [Blackpool Classrooms that Communicate toolkit](https://sites.google.com/thespeechbubble.co.uk/bctc)

## Specialist

- Advice from a speech and language therapist on appropriate interventions approaches for teaching and learning
- Advice about alternative, augmentative communication (AAC) available through Blackpool Council SEND support team
- Access to [Blackpool Classrooms that Communicate toolkit](https://sites.google.com/thespeechbubble.co.uk/bctc)
- Access to further information on alternative, augmentative communication (AAC) from the [ACE Centre](https://acecentre.org.uk/).
