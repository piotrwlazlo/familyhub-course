# Advisory and young people's voice groups in Blackpool
URL: https://www.blackpool.gov.uk/Residents/Education-and-schools/Local-offer/Young-people/Advisory-and-young-peoples-voice-groups-in-Blackpool.aspx

These are some of the groups for young people and adults where they can be part of having a voice for young people with SEND in Blackpool.

## Youth Empowerment forum

The Forum is for young people aged 9 to 25.

- Do you want your voice to be heard?
- Do you want to help us make decisions?

The young empowerment forums make positive changes to services in Blackpool for other people with SEND. The group meets every school term in one of our Blackpool schools.

We do all sorts of exciting work in our meetings, and out and about in the town. The YEF develop resources for children and young people, train professionals, and more!

If you would like to join our group, and/or are a professional keen to get some of your SEND children and young people involved, please contact the children’s engagement lead on the details below.

Email: [sendsupport.team@blackpool.gov.uk](mailto:sendsupport.team@blackpool.gov.uk)

## Blackpool Youth Council for 11 to 18 year olds

Blackpool Youth Council are involved in activities and campaigns that enable them to make positive changes for young people in Blackpool. We work with different organisations and partners across the town to help shape and decision makers that affect the lives of local young people.

Blackpool Youth Council are aware of the needs of individuals or groups of young people who may experience disadvantage or are unable to speak for themselves. Their aim is to raise the profile of young people in a positive way.

Wednesdays, 5.00 to 7.00pm

Email: [youthservice@blackpool.gov.uk](mailto:youthservice@blackpool.gov.uk)
