# Support available at Blackpool family hub
URL: https://www.blackpool.gov.uk/Residents/Education-and-schools/Early-years-and-childcare/Family-Hubs-and-Start-for-Life/Family-Hubs/Support-available-at-Blackpool-Family-Hub.aspx

If you’re struggling with your mental health, Blackpool family hubs and our partners are here to support you. Here are a range of services below that you can access.

## Advice and helplines

- **Mind** – a national charity that gives advice on mental health. Phone: 0300 123 3393. Email: [info@mind.org.uk](mailto:info@mind.org.uk) [Mind website](https://www.mind.org.uk/)
- **Childline** – if you’re under 19, you can call the Childline mental health support line. The number won’t appear on your phone bill. Phone: 0800 1111
- **Every Mind Matters** – a free mental health quiz that creates a personalised plan, with tips and advice to help you look after your mental health and wellbeing. [Take the Every Mind Matters quiz](https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/)
- **Samaritans –** The Samaritans is a charity dedicated to preventing suicide. Whatever you're going through, a Samaritan will face it with you. They're available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Just call 116 123.

## Support for young people's mental health

- **The Mix -** We connect young people to experts and their peers to talk about everything from money to mental health, homelessness to jobs, break-ups to drugs and more. No topic is out of bounds, and we are completely non-judgemental. [www.themix.org.uk](http://www.themix.org.uk/)
- **NSPCC –** the NSPCC has a hub of resources for parents to balance their mental health, dealing with toddler tantrums and recognising signs of self-harm in young people. [www.nspcc.org.uk/advice-for-families/](https://www.nspcc.org.uk/advice-for-families/)
- **Young Minds -** YoungMinds is a mental health charity for children, young people and their parents, making sure all young people can get the mental health support they deserve. [www.youngminds.org.uk](https://www.youngminds.org.uk)
- **Beat Eating Disorders -** Eating disorders are serious mental illnesses, and if you think you may have one, you deserve help to get better. Learn more about how to ask for and what to expect from treatment, as well as how we can support you. [www.beateatingdisorders.org.uk](http://www.beateatingdisorders.org.uk/)
- **Soluna (by Kooth) -** Soluna by Kooth offers free, safe confidential mental health support for young people aged 10 to 25. Soluna is available as web-version and a mobile app (for over 13s), which can be accessed at: [https://solunaapp.com/uk](https://solunaapp.com/uk) or via the App Store/Play Store.

## Talking therapies and groups

- **Blackpool Talking Therapies** – free psychological therapies for people aged 16 and over.
- **Lancashire Women** – a local charity that runs talking therapy sessions for women in Blackpool. Wellbeing sessions are held in our hubs by referral only. Phone: 0300 330 1354 . Lancashire Women website: lancashirewomen.org
- **Elliot’s Place** – a space for men, run by local men, to talk about mental health. It works to reduce the stigma that still surrounds mental health conversations, particularly in male-dominated environments, and encourages men to reach out and speak honestly about how life is for them.

## Support after abuse or bereavement

- **Birchall Trust** – counselling and support for survivors of rape, sexual abuse and sexualised violence, including adult counselling and specialist therapists for young people. Professionals can refer through the Birchall Trust website. Sessions can be held in our hubs. Email: [enquiries@birchalltrust.org.uk](mailto:enquiries@birchalltrust.org.uk). [Birchall Trust website.](https://www.birchalltrust.org.uk/)
- **Solace** – compassionate support for anyone affected by suicide or suspected suicide, with a varied weekly programme of support and activities. [See the Solace weekly programme.](https://empowermentcharity.org.uk/im-looking-for-help/suicide-prevention-bereavement)
- **The Den** – provides specialist, trauma-informed support to children and young people aged 3 years and above affected by domestic abuse. [Contact them here.](https://empowermentcharity.org.uk/im-looking-for-help/domestic-abuse)

## Pregnancy and new parents

- **Tommy’s** – a charity offering mental health support and advice for women during pregnancy. Phone: 0800 014 7800. Email: [midwife@tommys.org](mailto:midwife@tommys.org). [Tommy’s mental health and wellbeing](https://www.tommys.org/pregnancy-information/planning-a-pregnancy/planning-a-pregnancy-and-mental-illness/treatments-severe-mental-illness-during-and-after-pregnancy)
- **Mind Your Step walks** – walks run by the hubs with a trained mental health professional. A relaxed place to talk with other new parents and share your experiences and feelings.

See when the next Mind Your Step walk is on by looking at our [timetable.](https://www.blackpool.gov.uk/Residents/Education-and-schools/Early-years-and-childcare/Family-Hubs-and-Start-for-Life/Family-Hubs/Family-Hubs-timetable.aspx)

- **Move and Meet walks** – Active Blackpool is running a six-week walking course for pregnant women and new mums with children up to two years old. Each one-hour session includes a gentle, guided walk in the park led by a midwifery health trainer, friendly conversation, and advice on pregnancy, wellbeing, nutrition, exercise, routines and sleep. Join the sessions that suit you – you don’t need to come every week. Places are limited. To book, text MOVE followed by your name to 07467 444656
