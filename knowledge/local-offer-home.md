## Neu Way Support

Launch of new support service for families of children and young people with neurodevelopmental needs. We are delighted to inform you that from 6 July, families across Lancashire and South Cumbria will be able to access Neu Way Support, a new service for families of children and young people aged 0 to 25 who may have autism, ADHD or other neurodevelopmental differences. The service is available based on need, which means you do not need a diagnosis to get support.

Neu Way Support is available for families of children and young people aged 0 to 25 who:

- Have concerns about autism, ADHD or other neurodevelopmental differences
- Are waiting for an assessment
- Have recently received a diagnosis
- Would benefit from information, advice or support to help navigate services

[Neu Way Support, Spring North website](https://springnorth.org.uk/projects/neu-way-support/)

## Learning Disability Partnership Carers Group

The Learning Disability Partnership Board are looking into whether carers want a dedicated group specifically for those caring for someone with a learning disability. It would be both a place to get support, and a place to ensure that carers have a voice in shaping the work of the Learning Disability Partnership Board.

The adult social care team at [Blackpool Council have put together a survey to ask about this](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/e/QKW7F7urBC). The more responses they get, the clearer picture they will have of what is needed.
