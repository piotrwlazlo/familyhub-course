## Have your say

We need your voice to help us provide the best for you and your family and to help us shape the services offered through our family hubs and Best Start in Life offer.

Our parent and carer panels offer the chance to come together and share experiences on different services and activities for families in your local community.

The panels are currently made up of parents with children in the Best Start in Life period, aged 0 to 2, but parents of children aged 0 to 19 are also welcome to join!

The feedback collected from parents that use our family hubs is used to shape change. In the past this has been to change the layout of our timetables, the way that our maternity services are run and even to shape our parent volunteer programme.

These are informal meet-ups at each of our hubs, where you can have a free brew and a natter with our parent carer panel coordinator. These are also a great way to provide feedback to our staff in a laid-back way. Other parents love these sessions as a way to connect with other local families, talk about common parenting experiences and putting the world to rights!

We link these up to some of our activity sessions so they’re a great opportunity to dip your toes into our hub offer.

These informal meetings take place once a month and we provide brews and sandwiches. We simply ask attendees for their feedback on the sessions we provide, the support we offer and how we can improve our offer to local families. We will occasionally host informal focus groups and surveys as part of this. Hearing your voices is at the heart of everything we do in our family hubs.

We host these panels each month, rotating across areas of town. These meetings take place in person however, we’re currently looking at hosting one of these sessions online.

## Who can come along?

We know that in Blackpool the term "parent" or "carer" can cover a wide range of people. We would love to invite you if you are a:

- Mum
- Dad
- Carers
- Grandparents
- Kinship carers
- If you’re expecting a baby in the family
- Have responsibility for a child under two

Or if none of those descriptions fit, and your child accesses a session or you access support at Blackpool Family Hubs, get in touch to chat with our parent carer panel coordinator Hannah if you would like to be involved.

Email on [Hannah.Clark@blackpool.gov.uk](mailto:Hannah.Clark@blackpool.gov.uk) or text 07385 002 813.

Blackpool Family Hubs offers volunteer roles for Blackpool residents. With our own dedicated staff member, there are plenty of opportunities for you to give back to your community and get involved in the work of the hubs.

Volunteers contribute in a variety of ways, including supporting sessions, assisting with events and activities, helping in hub cafés and promoting services within the community.

Whether volunteers are looking to gain new skills, build confidence, or simply give back to their local community, volunteering with Family Hubs offers a rewarding opportunity to make a positive impact while being part of a supportive and friendly team.

If you are interested or know someone who might want to become a Family Hub volunteer, we would love to hear from you.
