# Blackpool SEND strategy 2024 to 2027
URL: https://www.blackpool.gov.uk/Residents/Education-and-schools/Local-offer/Strategy-and-policies/Blackpool-SEND-strategy-2022-to-2025.aspx

Welcome to the Blackpool Special Educational Needs and Disabilities (SEND) Strategy which sets out our key priorities and actions to help children and young people with SEND between the ages of 0 to 25 years and their families, live happy, healthy lives in Blackpool.

This strategy is the result of our SEND Partnership Board working together and listening to the views and experiences of parents/carers, children and young people, and professionals across education, health and social care.
