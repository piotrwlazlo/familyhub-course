# Family Hubs and Start for Life
URL: https://www.blackpool.gov.uk/Residents/Education-and-schools/Early-years-and-childcare/Family-Hubs-and-Start-for-Life/Family-Hubs-and-Start-for-Life.aspx

Blackpool Council's children's centres have now grown into family hubs.

There are three hubs across Blackpool offering a variety of services for families with children aged zero to 19 and up to 25 for children with special educational needs and disabilities (SEND).

To ensure that children in Blackpool have the best start in life, our family hubs are home to the Start for Life offer. This is a range of services that support the first 1,001 days of a child’s life which includes a number of fun activities [that can be found in our online timetable](https://www.blackpool.gov.uk/Residents/Education-and-schools/Early-years-and-childcare/Family-Hubs-and-Start-for-Life/Family-Hubs/Family-Hubs-timetable.aspx).
