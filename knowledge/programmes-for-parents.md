# Programmes for parents
URL: https://www.blackpool.gov.uk/Residents/Education-and-schools/Early-years-and-childcare/Family-Hubs-and-Start-for-Life/Family-Hubs/Programmes-for-parents.aspx

Bringing up children isn’t easy. All parents need support at times with their role as a parent. Your local family hub can help and support you with all areas of raising children.

We run a variety of programmes for families with children of all ages and needs, take a look at the session descriptions below. Or call in and chat to one of our friendly family hub staff to find out more about the range of free programmes available.

Triple P stands for “positive parenting programme” and is a toolkit of practical strategies designed to help families build strong healthy relationships and manage every-day behaviour with confidence. Instead of telling you how to raise children, Triple P gives families a menu of ideas so they can choose what works best for their family.

You can request to sign up to the next available course by getting in touch with the family hubs.

Triple P for baby

Are you a new parent? Join us for our dedicated Baby course. This nine-week programme provides friendly and relaxed sessions that will help you to promote your baby’s development, build a strong bond with your baby, and form stronger relationships with the people helping you care for baby.

Meet other new parents/carers in a similar position, this could be a really great way to start your journey as a family.

This programme is available face to face or online. Get in touch with your local family hub to sign up for the next course.

Triple P for children

Are you a parent or carer of a pre-school or school-aged child? Join us for our Triple P for Children programme (2 to 12 years). This nine-week programme provides tips and ideas to help bring out the best in your child, whilst also positively managing common emotional and behavioural difficulties as they grow up.

Come along and meet other parents and carers who are in a similar situation, whilst you navigate raising your pre-school and school aged children.

This programme is available face to face only. Get in touch with your local family hub to sign up for the next course.

Triple P teen

Are you a parent or carer of a teenager? Join us for our Triple P Teen programme. This nine-week programme offers laid back sessions which provide tips and ideas to help bring out the best in your teenager.

As your child grows into a teenager and through-out the teenage years, things can feel difficult, but we will give you the skills to positively manage common teenage emotional and behavioural difficulties. Come along and meet other parents or carers who are in a similar situation whilst you navigate bringing up a teenager.

Nation joined our Triple P teen programme and said "The ladies running the session helped me to be confident in 'laying down the law'. I also learned it's better for my son's independence if I don't run around after him like a maid. It's meant that home feels more calm, and I am less stressed. The sessions feel like I'm just coming to chat to some friends, I've loved it!"

This programme is available face to face only. Get in touch with your local family hub to sign up for the next course.

Triple P stepping stones (for parents of children with additional needs aged 2 to 12 years)

Are you a parent or carer of a child with additional needs? Join us for our Triple P Stepping Stones programme. This ten-week programme offers friendly and relaxed sessions which provide practical guidance for you in caring for a child with additional needs (2 to 12 years).

Parents have told us that they particularly value the opportunity to meet with other parents and carers who can share in the unique challenges that you face.

This programme is available face to face only. Get in touch with your local family hub to sign up for the next course.

Triple P fear-less

If your child is experiencing anxiety, you could join us for our new Triple P Fear-Less Programme. This seven-week programme is designed to help you in supporting your child to cope with their anxiety.

We will help you develop a range of tools and skills you can use to help them with overcoming their anxiety. Parents may find this particularly useful for children who are worried about going to school, friendships or other fears in life.

This programme is available face to face only. Get in touch with your local family hub to sign up for the next course.

Empowering parents, empowering communities (EPEC for short)

Parenting can be challenging, but you don’t have to do it alone! Empowering parents, empowering communities (EPEC) Being a Parent is a welcoming group for parents, carers, and family members of children under 12.

This group is unique because it is run by two trained local Blackpool parents who bring their own experiences and skills. In each session, you can expect a friendly welcome and supportive conversations to empower yourself and those around you. These sessions uniquely focus on nurturing you as a parent and making sure you look after yourself to benefit the whole family..

Over nine weekly sessions, you’ll explore key topics like communication, listening skills, discipline, and managing family stress. Sessions are available both face-to-face and online, so you can choose which option works best for you.

This programme is available face to face and online. Get in touch with your local family hub to sign up for the next course.

Relationships matter

Are you a parent or carer who would like to improve communication with your partner or ex-partner? Join us for our Relationships Matter course where we explore the different stages of a relationship, equip you with skills to overcome conflict and build healthier communication skills.

Come along and find out all about argument styles and how you can disagree in a healthier way, which will improve your family life. We welcome both partners who are together and those who are separated and want to co-parent in a positive way for the benefit of their children.

Parents tell us they have built stronger relationships and get along better after attending these sessions.

This programme is available face to face only. Get in touch with your local family hub to sign up for the next course.
