# Blackpool SEND support team offer to schools and colleges
URL: https://www.blackpool.gov.uk/Residents/Education-and-schools/Local-offer/Primary-and-secondary/Blackpool-SEND-support-team-offer-to-schools-and-colleges.aspx

All schools within Blackpool are allocated a dedicated Advisory Teacher who works collaboratively with SENCOs, school leaders and staff to identify priorities, plan support, and develop inclusive practice across the setting.

Advisory Teachers and SEND Advisory Officers provide strategic advice, guidance and practical support in a range of areas, including:

- SENCO supervision and professional support
- Development and implementation of the Graduated Approach across all areas of SEND
- Supporting the identification and assessment of pupils with SEND
- Advising on SEND provision planning and effective responses to pupil needs
- Supporting SENCOs with SEND action planning, provision mapping, assessments and interventions
- Delivery of staff meetings, professional development and training
- Identifying and addressing emerging themes and trends relating to SEND within schools
- Supporting priority year groups, classes or cohorts through observations, learning walks and recommendations to enhance learning environments and classroom practice

The team offers specialist support across the following areas of need:

- Communication and Interaction
- Cognition and Learning
- Social, Emotional and Mental Health (SEMH), including trauma-informed approaches
- Alternative and Augmentative Communication (AAC) and Assistive Technology

In addition to providing SEND-focused advice and guidance, Advisory Teacher link meetings offer valuable professional support for SENCOs. This collaborative approach helps promote consistency across Blackpool schools, fostering a shared understanding of SEND, effective identification of needs, and the use of assessment to inform high-quality provision and intervention.
