## Art at the Start

Join Art at the Start, a playful and creative experience that explores how art can support your baby’s wellbeing, development, and early relationships. Through gentle, hands-on activities, you and your baby can enjoy special moments of connection while creating together.

Making art side by side encourages bonding, confidence, and self-expression, helping babies learn that their actions are valued and celebrated. These shared experiences become the building blocks for strong attachments and positive self-esteem in a nurturing environment.

Suitable for children and babies aged 0 to 4 years

Where and when:
