# Blackpool SENCO network meetings
URL: https://www.blackpool.gov.uk/Residents/Education-and-schools/Local-offer/Professionals/Blackpool-SENCO-network-meetings.aspx

The SENCO Network is firmly established as a resource providing regular updates, expert advice from guest speakers and interactive discussion to enable SENCOs to develop effective practice.

Because the SENCO role is unique within schools, it may feel isolating. Meetings offer the chance to share concerns, get advice on specific issues and identify good practice with colleagues.

The SENCO Network meets half termly for face-to-face meetings from 2.30pm to 4.00pm on the following dates:

- Thursday 8 October 2026, 2.30pm to 4.00pm
- Thursday 3 December 2026, 2.30pm to 4.00pm
- Tuesday 9 February 2027, 2.30pm to 4.00pm
- Wednesday 17 March 2027, 2.30pm to 4.00pm
- Tuesday 4 May 2027, 2.30pm to 4.00pm
- Thursday 1 July 2027, 2.30pm to 4.00pm

There is a SENCO Network teams resource folder where all meeting slides are held and resources are shared.

If you would like to be added to the distribution list and receive invites for SENCO Network meetings or any queries regarding the SENCO Network contact: [SENDSupport.team@blackpool.gov.uk](mailto:SENDSupport.team@blackpool.gov.uk)
