## Information for parents and carers about what is available in Blackpool for children and young people with special educational needs and disabilities, and their families.

## What is SEND?

A child or young person has SEND if they have a learning difficulty or disability which means they need special educational provision to be made for them.

A child of compulsory school age or a young person has a learning difficulty or disability if they:

- Have a significantly greater difficulty in learning than the majority of others of the same age, or
- Have a disability which prevents or hinders them from making use of facilities of a kind generally provided for others of the same age in mainstream schools or mainstream post-16 institutions.

Professionals who work with children, young people and families with SEND follow guidelines from the SEND code of practice.


## At home library service

Our At Home library service is available for Blackpool residents who are unable to visit their local library due to illness, age or disability.

We can deliver library books and audio books to your home.

The service is based at Palatine library. Items are chosen by library staff to suit individual taste, they are delivered and collected once every 3 weeks on a set day as arranged by one of our library volunteers.

If you would like to take advantage of this service, or know of someone who would, please contact Palatine Library on 01253 478050 or email: [athomelibrary@blackpool.gov.uk](mailto:athomelibrary@blackpool.gov.uk)

### You choose we read library service

You can request a selection of up to 6 books, specially chosen by library staff, for collection from your local Blackpool library.

You can specify the types of books you like to read and/or receive surprise recommendations.

To do this you need to complete the We Choose, You Read form, and we will let you know when they are ready to collect.

You need to be a library member to use this free service. [If you aren’t a member, you can join now](https://blackpool.spydus.co.uk/cgi-bin/spydus.exe/MSGTRN/OPAC/JOIN).

Alternatively, if you wish to reserve a specific title, please log in to your [online account to place a reservation](https://blackpool.spydus.co.uk/cgi-bin/spydus.exe/MSGTRN/OPAC/LOGINB).
