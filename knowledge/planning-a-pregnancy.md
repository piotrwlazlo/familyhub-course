# Planning a pregnancy
URL: https://www.blackpool.gov.uk/residents/education-and-schools/early-years-and-childcare/family-hubs-and-best-start-for-life/best-start-for-life/planning-a-pregnancy

## Advice and support for parents-to-be

If you are planning a pregnancy:

- Taking [folic acid](https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/trying-for-a-baby/planning-your-pregnancy/) helps to reduce the risk of problems as your baby develops in the early weeks. In Blackpool, you can get free pregnancy vitamins from your [local Family Hub](/residents/education-and-schools/early-years-and-childcare/family-hubs-and-best-start-for-life/family-hubs/blackpool-family-hubs).
- It is the best time to make other lifestyle changes too, such as stopping smoking. Advice for this is available via the [NHS Stop Smoking Services](https://www.bfwh.nhs.uk/our-services/stop-smoking-service/) and support from your local Family Hub is available
- It is key to not drink alcohol during pregnancy. Advice and support on this is available [via the NHS website](https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/drinking-alcohol-while-pregnant/), or visit your local Family Hub for support
- If you are looking for tips on how to stay healthy during your pregnancy, you can get advice on [healthy diet on the NHS website](https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/have-a-healthy-diet/) and for tips on staying active, find out more about [Active Blackpool](https://www.blackpool.gov.uk/residents/sports-and-leisure/home/healthy-lifestyles/active-blackpool.aspx)

If you’re thinking about having a baby, visit [Blackpool Better Start’s page](https://blackpoolbetterstart.org.uk/pregnant-under1/baby-steps/) for advice.

There are things you can start doing now to improve the health of your future pregnancy and children. A great place to start to find out what will help you is [Tommy's Tool](https://www.tommys.org/pregnancy-information/planning-pregnancy/planning-for-pregnancy-tool).

For more information, you can also visit the NHS website and find out more through [Start4Life - Pregnancy](https://www.nhs.uk/start4life/pregnancy/).
