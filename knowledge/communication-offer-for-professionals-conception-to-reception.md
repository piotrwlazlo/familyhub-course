# Communication offer for professionals - Conception to Reception

Last Modified October 13, 2025

## Universal

- Access to key messages promoting positive interaction from the peri-natal stage
- Brazelton Newborn Observation/Hello Baby training offered to all members of the health visitors team
- Communication champion cluster 3 times per year.
- Support to work towards the Blackpool Speech Language and Communication Framework 
- Individual support and advice from the commissioned speech and language therapy service
- Speech and language UK training in Early Talk and Early Talk Boost
- DfE Early Child Development Training 
- Early years professional development 
- Training in use of WellComm assessment tool
- Support from a speech, language and communication advisory teacher 
- Monthly surgery in setting
- Early Years settings offered support related to work force development
- Makaton training via NHS speech and language therapist payable by settings
- Access to the annual North West Speech and Language Network [Information Day](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/849369415787?aff=oddtdtcreator)

## Targeted

- Referral to specialist health visitor for Perinatal and Infant Relationship Service (PAIRS) for advice/support/training
- Access to advice for all children with identified delay, if triaged to setting support
- Access to Early Talk  and Early Talk Boost intervention training
- Single point of entry for multi-agency triage panel
- Practitioners to have access to key messages promoting positive interaction from the early years team, Communicate CIC and Better Start
- One funded NHS Makaton training place for staff, per child with identified need

## Specialist

- Advice and support for the implementation of speech and language therapy targets within the setting
- Access to early years SEND advisory teacher support for children with complex speech, language and communication
- Needs led training e.g. for stammering, selective mutism etc.
- Acces to further information on alternative, augmentative communication (AAC) through the [ACE Centre](https://acecentre.org.uk/).
