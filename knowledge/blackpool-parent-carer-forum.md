# Blackpool Parent Carer Forum
URL: https://www.blackpool.gov.uk/Residents/Education-and-schools/Local-offer/Parents/Blackpool-Parent-Carer-Forum.aspx

Blackpool Parent Carer Forum are a volunteer-led group of SEND parents funded by the department of education consisting of:

- Chair- Emma Wright
- Co-chair- Leanne Errington
- Steering group members and volunteers

We offer peer support and engagement opportunities for families of children and young people aged 0 to 25 with diagnosed or undiagnosed additional needs.

We are parents on our own journeys and we understand.

## Joining the Parent Carer Forum

## Support the Parent Carer Forum offer

- Peer support from people who “live it everyday”
- Events and drop-ins
- A safe space to connect and ask questions
- Signposting and advice tailored to your child’s needs
- Form filling support
- A listening ear when you need it most
- Engagement opportunities with services

## Services the Parent Carer Forum work with

We work closely with:

- Blackpool Council
- Health services (NHS)
- Social care & early help
- Mental health support providers
- SENDIASS
- Makaton specialists
- Voluntary and private sector organisations
- Educational settings

Together, we aim to improve services and outcomes for SEND families.

## Goals of the Parent Carer Forum

We are passionate about:

- Making sure families are heard
- Building strong partnerships with local services
- Creating change based on lived experience
- Helping every child reach their full potential
- Ensuring no parent feels alone or invisible
- Myth busting for parents - we answer questions with facts and through the forum we can take any questions/ concerns to professionals and support to build relationships

## How Parent Carer Forum create change

Listening to parent experiences

- Feeding insights into decision-making
- Co-producing better services
- Highlighting what’s working (and what’s not)
- Bringing professionals and families together
- Collate data to find gaps within the SEND systems

## Why the Parent Carer Forum is important for services

You can help us:

- Signpost families to the forum
- Share our events and drop-ins
- Let us know about any emerging needs
- Invite us to meet families at your sessions
- Bridge the gap between families and services
- We could support you to make your sessions SEND friendly
